What I do and believe

I'm a practitioner, not a guru. By building successful product organizations, I've dramatically impacted the bottom line of the companies I've worked with. I'm here to help you achieve your goals through impactful interim product leadership, coaching and advisory.

I coach and advise founders, executives, product leaders and teams, focusing on product strategy, culture, cross-functional structures and lean processes — with one goal in mind: to make your product org excel at building the best products in the market.

The industry is changing towards a new, exciting model of product leadership: going away from stakeholder-led project management disguised as product management, the shift towards empowering teams to focus on mission-critical, high-value outcomes brings about much-needed disruption – but also new challenges in transforming towards the new model. I've helped multiple companies through this transition in a sustainable, cost-effective way that ensures lasting success. I look forward to getting to know you and your challenges!

Pain points I've faced

  • No product/market fit, slow growth
  • Focus on activities, not outcomes
  • Lack of alignment between teams
  • Too many things going on that don't move the needle

Strategies I've used

  • Strong focus with sharp product strategy
  • Empowered, mission-driven teams that create outcomes
  • Cross-functional, highly aligned teams
  • Experimental, fun, fail-fast culture

Outcomes created

  • Vastly improved growth & business metrics
  • Better & more focused products – delivered faster
  • Clear vision & strategy, high team engagement
  • Higher eNPS, reduced churn, more ambitious goal setting
