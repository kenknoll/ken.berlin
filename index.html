What I do and believe

I'm a practitioner, not a guru. Whether I'm brought in by a PE operating partner to turn a value-creation plan into a delivered product roadmap, or by a founder building their first empowered product team, I've dramatically impacted the bottom line of the organizations I've worked with. I'm here to help you achieve your company's goals through impactful fractional and interim product leadership, coaching and advisory.

I lead and operate inside PE-backed portfolio companies, working hands-on with leadership teams to deliver the plan, not just advise on it. I bring the same operating discipline as coach and advisor to founders and product leaders building outside that context. The focus is product strategy, culture, cross-functional structures and lean processes, with one goal in mind: building the right products, and the operating model behind them, that sustainably realize the growth plan.

The industry is shifting decisively towards outcome-focused product leadership, away from stakeholder-led project management disguised as product management. Building software is no longer the bottleneck; building the right product is, and speed only counts if empowered teams actually deliver outcomes customers and the business need. In an increasingly AI-driven market, that means better outcomes through better, data-informed decisions, not lock-in through scope. I've helped multiple companies make this transition sustainably, including turning a PE fund's value-creation plan into a product organization that delivers it, AI strategy included. You can read more on how I approach AI-native transformation. I look forward to getting to know you and your challenges!

Pain points I've faced

  • No AI strategy while competitors reposition around it
  • Delivery and impact that don't scale with the growth plan
  • No clear, coherent strategy tied to the company's overall plan
  • Focus on activities, not outcomes
  • Lack of alignment between teams

Strategies I've used

  • Strong focus with sharp product strategy
  • Empowered, mission-driven teams that create outcomes
  • Cross-functional, highly aligned teams
  • AI product strategy focused on better decisions, not lock-in
